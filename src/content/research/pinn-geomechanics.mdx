# Physics-Informed Neural Networks in Geomechanics

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) embed physical laws—such as conservation of momentum and mass—directly into the loss function of neural networks.

## Abstract
Traditional data-driven models require large amounts of experimental or simulation data. PINNs reduce this requirement by constraining the search space using governing geomechanical equations. or simulation data.
